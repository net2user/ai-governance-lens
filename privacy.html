Privacy Policy - AI Governance Lens

Last updated: August 2026

AI Governance Lens is a Chrome extension that answers questions about RBI AI governance guidance.

What we collect

When you submit a question, it is sent to our backend service for processing and may be logged with a timestamp for usage tracking. We do not collect names, emails, or any personally identifiable information. We do not use cookies or track you across websites.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your queries with third parties. We do not display ads.

Third party services

Questions are processed using Groq's language model API to generate answers grounded in regulatory documents.
